Designing a wind-tunnel test: a 1:N model that flies like the real aircraft

A scale model is only a valid stand-in for the full-scale aircraft if the flow over it is dynamically similar — same geometry and α, same Reynolds number Re = ρVc/μ, and same Mach number. Get those right and the measured cl and cd transfer to the aircraft unchanged.
